Mandatory Site Safety Induction

Complete this brief prior to entering active work zones.

1. Core Site Rules
  • Full PPE (Hard hat, high-vis, safety boots, glasses) required at all times beyond the gate.
  • Sign in digitally or at the main site office before commencing any work.
  • Report all near-misses and incidents immediately to the Site Manager.
2. Environmental & Access Constraints
  • Strict 10mph speed limit across the entire site footprint.
  • No unauthorized entry into excavation or utility buffer zones.
Emergency Procedure & Muster Point:
In the event of an alarm or site evacuation, immediately drop tools and report to the **Main Gate Compound Muster Point**. Do not re-enter until an all-clear is given.
